Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Zdrilich Web Studios ("we", "our" or "us") operates the Neon Gravity mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

Neon Gravity does not collect, store or transmit personally identifiable data. The game runs entirely on the user's device.

The only information stored locally on the device is:

  • Game scores and progress
  • Game preferences
  • Unlocked ships
  • In-app purchase status

This data is stored exclusively on the user's device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to personalize ads. For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: policies.google.com/privacy

Users can remove advertising by making the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.

Apple App Store

In-app purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. For information about Apple's privacy practices, visit: apple.com/legal/privacy

Game Center

The application uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Game Center information is managed according to Apple's privacy policy.

Children's Data

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
